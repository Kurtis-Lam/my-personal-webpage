01 · About

A bit about me

Hey, I'm Kurtis. I live in Hong Kong, I'm still a student (currently G11 preparing for IB exams), and I like to code stuff because I enjoy it. I like projects where the results are simple yet useful.

I've shipped a Student Union website for my schools (voluntarily), a SVT based game, an IB study app, a wallpaper app for Huawei phones , a terminal coding assistant that runs open-source models on your own machine, a versatile Discord bot named Utilities, and lots more...

I also test things for vulnerabilities, explore new tech stuff, play around with LLMs, (and type on Monkeytype). If you have a problem that can be solved with code, I'd love to hear it and help out.
